Made for reading aloud

These stories use short sentences and words a young child knows. Sounds are repeated, the settings are familiar (a railway station, a tea cart, a school courtyard) and nothing frightening happens. If you are new to storytelling, read how to tell a bedtime story.

Questions people ask

Are these stories original?

Yes. Every story was written for this site. They are new stories, not retellings of Panchatantra, Aesop or any other collection.

How long are the stories?

Each story takes two to four minutes to read aloud, which suits bedtime and short attention spans. The reading time is shown on every story.

What does "Read aloud" do?

It asks your phone or computer to read the story in its own built-in voice, one paragraph at a time, and highlights the paragraph being read. Tap the button again to stop.

Can I share a story in my family group?

Yes. Tap "Send on WhatsApp" under a story to send its title, its moral and a link. Teachers may read the stories in class and print them for their students.
